Boards, lists, due dates, priorities, search, drag-and-drop — everything you'd expect from a real task manager. Use it for school, work, or just your own to-do list. And your tasks stay private, automatically, without you having to think about it.

Everything a task manager should do

Board and list views

Organize your tasks as a kanban board or a simple list — switch anytime, whatever fits how you're thinking that day.

Drag and drop

Move a task from To Do to In Progress to Done with a drag. No menus, no clicking through screens.

Due dates and priorities

Flag what's urgent, set a deadline, and know at a glance what needs your attention first.

Search and quick commands

Find any task in a second, or use the command menu to jump anywhere without touching your mouse.

Works offline

No internet? No problem. Haven runs right in your browser and saves everything on your own device.

Sync across devices

Want your tasks on your phone and your laptop? Turn on sync and use Haven anywhere — still private, same as always.

Your tasks are yours. Really.

Most apps can read everything you write, even if it's marked private. Haven can't. The moment you type a task, it gets locked on your own device before it's ever saved anywhere — so even we, the people who built this, can't read what you write.
