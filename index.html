Free task manager. No signup.

Track your tasks. Keep them to yourself.

Haven is a task manager with boards, lists, due dates, priorities, tags, and search — the stuff you'd expect from any to-do app. The difference is what happens after you hit save: everything you write gets encrypted on your own device first, so nobody else can read it. Not other users, not a database admin, not us.

A real task manager, not a one-trick demo

Board and list views

Some days a kanban board makes sense. Other days you just want a plain list. Haven gives you both, and switching between them takes one click.

Drag and drop

Drag a task from To Do to Done. That's the whole interaction — no dropdown, no separate screen to update its status.

Due dates and priorities

Set a due date, mark something high priority, and it's obvious at a glance what actually needs your attention today.

Search and quick commands

Type to search, or hit a key to pull up a command bar and jump anywhere in the app without touching your mouse.

Works offline

No wifi on the train? Doesn't matter. Haven runs entirely in your browser and keeps working with no connection at all.

Sync across devices

Turn on sync if you want your tasks on your phone too. It's optional, and it's still private on every device.

The part most task apps skip

Most "private" apps just mean nobody outside the company can see your data. The company still can. Haven works differently: your tasks are scrambled on your own device before they're ever saved, using the same kind of encryption banks use for financial data. We built it, and we still can't read what you write — not because we promise not to, but because we don't have a way to. Type something below and see it happen for yourself.
