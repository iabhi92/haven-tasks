01 / The task manager

Get the important stuff out of your head.

Haven gives you a simple place to put everything you need to remember, from today's errands to the project you've been putting off for three weeks.

01 — Today

See what's due, without digging

Today, Upcoming, and Overdue smart views surface what needs attention without hunting through every project.

02 — Projects

Keep work, study, and life separate

Multiple boards and projects, so a work sprint and a grocery list never end up in the same list.

03 — Priorities

Know what needs attention first

High, medium, low — sort by priority or due date so the important thing doesn't get buried.

04 — Recurring

Set it once. Let Haven remember it.

Daily, weekly, or monthly recurring tasks spawn their next occurrence automatically when you finish one.

05 — Search

Find the thing you know you wrote down

Instant search across every task and project — parsed on your device, never sent anywhere to be indexed.

06 — Boards

Drag, drop, done

A real kanban board and list view, bulk actions with undo, and JSON export/import whenever you want your data out.

Also included: multiple projects, drag-and-drop boards, bulk actions with undo, and optional cross-device sync. Your data is yours to export whenever you need it.

You shouldn't need an account to remember something.

Most apps ask you to create an account before they'll let you write down a task. Haven doesn't. Open it, add your tasks, keep going — your task list is yours, and you shouldn't need to hand over your email address just to make one.

02 / The part most task apps skip

Your task list says more about you than you think.

A task list doesn't look particularly sensitive — until it contains your doctor's appointment, a client's name, a job you're applying for, a personal reminder, or something you simply don't want sitting on somebody else's server.

Haven encrypts your tasks on your device before it's saved. The server receives the encrypted version, not the readable task. The encryption key stays with you, so we don't have a readable copy of what you write.

We built Haven. We still can't read your tasks.

Haven isn't for everyone.

      
    
        
  • No native mobile app yet — Haven runs great as a mobile web page, but there's no App Store/Play Store listing today.
    • 
        
  • Smaller ecosystem — no calendar integrations, no plugin marketplace, none of the years of polish Todoist or Things have.
    • 
        
  • No large-scale team collaboration — Haven is built for one person's tasks first.
    • 
        
  • Passphrase recovery has real consequences — lose your passphrase and your recovery code, and nobody, including us, can get your data back.
